# File Directory: papers/P004.pdf

## 1. Metadata
* **Authors & Year:** Alireza Dehghan, Negin Ashrafi (2026)
* **Publication Venue:** arXiv (Preprint / Submitted Manuscript)
* **Target Population & Region:** Medical and non-medical university students | Lausanne, Switzerland
* **Sample Size ($N$):** 886 (Primary cohort, 2022) validated against 2,580 longitudinal observations and 701 non-medical students.

## 2. Core Focus & Psychological Metrics
* **Primary Objective:** To audit construct overlap in explainable machine learning (XML) pipelines applied to burnout-depression prediction, demonstrating that apparent cross-population stability in XAI risk hierarchies is largely a psychometric artefact rather than a learned generalisable risk structure.
* **Burnout / Mental Health Assessment Tool:** Maslach Burnout Inventory–Student Survey (MBI-SS) for burnout; Center for Epidemiological Studies Depression Scale (CES-D) for depression; State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI-T) for trait anxiety.

## 3. Indicators & Feature Sets
* **Digital Wellbeing / Passive Indicators:** None used.
* **Self-Reported / Survey Indicators:** 10 demographic and psychological predictors including trait anxiety (STAI-T), health satisfaction, age, academic year, psychotherapy history, partnership status, employment status, weekly study hours, mother tongue, and sex. 

## 4. Machine Learning & Explainability (XAI)
* **ML Algorithms Evaluated:** Ordinary Least Squares (OLS), Ridge, Lasso, ElasticNet, Support Vector Regression (SVR), and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN).
* **Best Performing Model & Metrics:** ElasticNet (L1+L2 regularisation) was selected as the primary model, achieving an R² of 0.414 and MAE of 9.26 on the primary test set.
* **XAI / Interpretability Techniques Used:** SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) via `LinearExplainer`. Crucially, the study introduces a novel **"residualization protocol"** to audit SHAP results for construct overlap (i.e., isolating shared variance between correlated variables via regression).

## 5. Recommendations & Interventions
* **Personalized Recommendations Provided?** No algorithmic personalized recommendations are generated.
* **Actionable System Features:** Not an actionable intervention system. The study strictly concludes that individual-level clinical prediction is currently **not actionable** using these feature sets due to high uncertainty (conformal prediction intervals averaged 35.4 units on a 100-point scale). It recommends researchers mandate orthogonality checks (residualization) before interpreting feature hierarchies.

## 6. Critical Evaluation & Research Gaps
* **Main Strengths:** Exposes a critical methodological flaw in XAI mental health research (construct overlap); introduces a robust, mathematically sound residualization protocol; leverages comprehensive multi-cohort validation (longitudinal and cross-faculty).
* **Key Limitations:** Data strictly from a single institution (University of Lausanne) limits cross-institutional generalisability; the composite outcome index (BDCI) lacks independent psychometric validation; does not incorporate potentially critical predictors like financial stress or sleep quality.
* **Unaddressed Research Gaps:** Complete absence of physiological data or ecological momentary assessment (passive digital logging); lack of prospective designs testing cross-institutional variations; no exploration of diverse demographics or non-Western/LMIC contexts (e.g., Bangladesh).
