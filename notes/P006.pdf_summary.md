# File Directory: papers/P006.pdf

## 1. Metadata
* **Authors & Year:** Saima Alam, Mohammad Arif Ul Alam (2026)
* **Publication Venue:** JMIR AI
* **Target Population & Region:** Internal medicine resident physicians | United States (Massachusetts)
* **Sample Size ($N$):** 28 (primary clinical dataset); also evaluated on public datasets WESAD (n=15) and SWELL-KW (n=25).

## 2. Core Focus & Psychological Metrics
* **Primary Objective:** To develop and validate EMBRACE, an explainable multitask deep learning framework that predicts and explains future burnout in resident physicians using wearable sensor data and automatic clinical survey completion.
* **Burnout / Mental Health Assessment Tool:** Mini-Z Burnout Survey (a clinically validated 10-item tool assessing burnout, stress, and job satisfaction).

## 3. Indicators & Feature Sets
* **Digital Wellbeing / Passive Indicators:** Continuous wearable physiological data (heart rate variability, skin conductance/EDA, skin temperature, and 3-axis accelerometry) collected via the Empatica E4 wristband. Workplace behavioral metrics such as sedentary activity duration, time spent on EMR (Electronic Medical Records), and interruption frequency.
* **Self-Reported / Survey Indicators:** Mini-Z survey responses (perceived stress, control over workload, EMR stress, job satisfaction) and self-reported sleep quality.

## 4. Machine Learning & Explainability (XAI)
* **ML Algorithms Evaluated:** Adaptive Multitask Deep Learning (CNN + LSTM architecture) using Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning (MAML) for few-shot domain adaptation. Baselines included Bi-LSTM, Random Forest, Learning Vector Quantization, Gaussian Mixture Models, and Decision Trees.
* **Best Performing Model & Metrics:** EMBRACE (Adaptive Multitask Deep Learning) outperformed baselines. On the primary clinical dataset for burnout prediction, it achieved 94.7% Accuracy, 94.7% Precision, 94.7% Recall, and 95.1% F1-score.
* **XAI / Interpretability Techniques Used:** SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations). SHAP values were used to generate clinical visualizations, including feature importance plots and time-series summaries mapping daily activities to predicted stress levels.

## 5. Recommendations & Interventions
* **Personalized Recommendations Provided?** Yes. The EMBRACE framework automatically sends an end-of-day email to the resident. This email includes a personalized visual breakdown of their daily workload, burnout predictions, and specific recommendations to mitigate future burnout risks based on their unique SHAP feature importance (e.g., reducing EMR time or managing interruptions).
* **Actionable System Features:** Automated end-of-day email alerts, automated completion of the Mini-Z survey to save time, visual daily stress/activity summaries, and a web-based feedback loop for residents/clinicians to validate the model's predictions.

## 6. Critical Evaluation & Research Gaps
* **Main Strengths:** Highly innovative integration of passive physiological sensors with multitask deep learning to predict both burnout states and auto-complete clinical surveys; strong focus on clinical trustworthiness via SHAP-based email interventions; validated on both real-world clinical and public datasets.
* **Key Limitations:** Very small primary clinical sample size (n=28); short data collection window (2 to 7 days per participant); study conducted at a single US hospital, limiting broad generalizability.
* **Unaddressed Research Gaps:** Absence of long-term longitudinal studies to evaluate if the email-based interventions actually reduce burnout over months/years; lack of validation in diverse or resource-constrained global health systems (such as LMICs/Bangladesh); potential to include deeper social interaction metrics.
