# File Directory: papers/P011.pdf

## 1. Metadata
* **Authors & Year:** Mohammad Rezaur Rahman, Sayma Sharmen, Morshed Alam (2026)
* **Publication Venue:** Cogent Education
* **Target Population & Region:** Undergraduate private university students | Dhaka, Bangladesh
* **Sample Size ($N$):** 401

## 2. Core Focus & Psychological Metrics
* **Primary Objective:** To investigate the influence of Self-Evaluation Traits (SET) and Career Decision-Making Difficulties (CDMD) on student burnout, and to examine the mediating role of Social Support (SS) utilizing the Conservation of Resources (COR) theory.
* **Burnout / Mental Health Assessment Tool:** Maslach Burnout Inventory-Student Survey (MBI-SS) (used to measure emotional exhaustion, cynicism, and diminished accomplishment).

## 3. Indicators & Feature Sets
* **Digital Wellbeing / Passive Indicators:** None used.
* **Self-Reported / Survey Indicators:** 5-point Likert scale surveys measuring: Self-Evaluation Traits (12 items), Career Decision-Making Difficulties (8 items), Social Support (12 items), and Student Burnout (9 items). Demographic factors included gender, age, field of study, and year of study.

## 4. Machine Learning & Explainability (XAI)
* **ML Algorithms Evaluated:** None. The study employed traditional statistical modeling, specifically Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) via Smart PLS-4 and IBM SPSS.
* **Best Performing Model & Metrics:** The PLS-SEM path model demonstrated adequate predictive relevance and fit, explaining 26.6% of the variance in Student Burnout (R² = 0.266).
* **XAI / Interpretability Techniques Used:** None / Not Applicable. (Relies on traditional white-box PLS-SEM path coefficients and mediation analysis).

## 5. Recommendations & Interventions
* **Personalized Recommendations Provided?** No algorithmic personalized recommendations were provided.
* **Actionable System Features:** Not an actionable technological system. The study provides institutional policy recommendations, emphasizing the need for twofold interventions: (1) building organized social support networks (peer mentoring, faculty engagement) to mitigate situational career-decision stress, and (2) implementing self-efficacy training to maintain internal resilience.

## 6. Critical Evaluation & Research Gaps
* **Main Strengths:** Successfully addresses a major geographical gap by focusing on an LMIC context (private university students in Bangladesh); clarifies the distinct roles of internal resources (SET) versus external situational factors (CDMD) in predicting burnout; employs robust PLS-SEM methodology including common method bias and collinearity checks.
* **Key Limitations:** Cross-sectional design prevents causal conclusions; convenience sampling restricted to a single city (Dhaka) limits broader national or international generalizability.
* **Unaddressed Research Gaps:** Complete reliance on self-report questionnaires with no integration of objective behavioral markers or passive digital logging; absence of predictive Machine Learning (ML) models and explainable AI (XAI) frameworks; lacks longitudinal tracking to validate the suggested interventions over time.
